## Item 8 — Liquidity, Indebtedness, Covenants & Risk Factors (Note 14 & Item 1A)

- **Net Debt & Leverage:** As of December 31, 2025, Apex held **USD 88.0M** in 5.75% Senior Unsecured Notes due 2030 and **USD 23.5M** in unrestricted cash, yielding **Net Financial Debt of USD 64.5M** (**1.54x Adjusted EBITDA**).
- **Indenture Covenants:** Incurrence-based Consolidated Net Leverage covenant of **<= 3.50x Adjusted EBITDA** and Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio **>= 3.00x**; Change-of-Control put option at **101% of par** only upon a concurrent Dual-Agency Rating Downgrade below investment/crossover threshold.
- **Key Operational Risk Factor (Item 1A):** Single-source dependency on specialized rare-earth permanent magnet assemblies from East Asian suppliers representing **22% of Bill-of-Materials cost**, mitigated partially via 6-month buffer inventory.

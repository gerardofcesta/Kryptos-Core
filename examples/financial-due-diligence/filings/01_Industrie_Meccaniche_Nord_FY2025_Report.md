### Senior Facilities Agreement — Financial Covenants & Change-of-Control Trigger
1. **Net Leverage Maintenance Covenant:** Consolidated Net Financial Debt / Adjusted EBITDA tested semi-annually (30 June and 31 December) must not exceed **3.25x** for FY2025, stepping down to **2.75x** from 31 December 2026 onward. ## Section 3 — Tax, Legal & Environmental Contingencies (Note 24)

1. **FY2022 R&D Tax Credit Audit (Agenzia delle Entrate):** In October 2025, the Group received an assessment notice challenging **EUR 1.85M** of Industry 4.0 R&D tax credits claimed in FY2022, plus statutory interest. Management, supported by external tax counsel, assessed probable settlement exposure and recorded a **balance-sheet provision of EUR 0.60M**, leaving **EUR 1.25M as unprovided contingent tax exposure** requiring SPA specific tax indemnity escrow.
2. **Environmental Remediation Cap (Former Bergamo Foundry):** Under the 2021 asset divestiture deed, IMN retains residual groundwater monitoring obligations capped contractually at **EUR 2.50M** expiring December 2027 (EUR 0.40M environmental reserve currently accrued).
3. **Customer Concentration:** Top 3 German automotive/industrial Tier-1 customers account for **34.2% of FY2025 Consolidated Revenue**, governed by multi-year framework supply agreements without minimum volume guarantees.
